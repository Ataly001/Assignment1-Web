About David Goggins:

David Goggins is a retired Navy SEAL and ultramarathon runner. He lost over 100 pounds in 3 months to join the military. He is widely known for his mental toughness and bestselling book "Can't Hurt Me".

Important events:

  1. 1999 - Lost 100 lbs in 3 months to qualify for training
  2. 2001 - Graduated Navy SEAL training
  3. 2005 - Completed San Diego 100-mile ultramarathon
  4. 2013 - Set Guinness World Record for 4,030 pull-ups in 17 hours

Achievements:

  • Completed SEAL, Ranger, and Air Force training
  • Finished Badwater 135 ultramarathon
  • Created the 40% rule mindset
